# Document Intelligence

## The Business Problem

As your business grows, important information becomes spread across reports, contracts, policies, and other PDF documents. This can create common problems:

- Employees spend too much time opening files and searching page by page
- Important answers depend on the few people who know where information is stored
- Different document versions can lead to slow or inconsistent decisions
- Managers cannot quickly verify where an answer came from
- Repetitive document questions take time away from higher-value work
- Valuable information is available, but difficult to use when it is needed

These problems can slow daily work, increase dependence on individual employees, and make business decisions harder to verify.

## Key Features

- **Ask your documents:** Ask questions in plain language and get concise answers from uploaded PDFs.
- **Verify every answer:** Citations link answers to the source file and page, making evidence easy to check.
- **Find more relevant information:** Hybrid search combines meaning-based and keyword search across text, tables, and scanned pages.
- **Manage knowledge centrally:** Upload, categorize, track, and remove documents from one knowledge base.

## Potential ROI

Document Intelligence reduces time spent searching files and repeatedly asking subject-matter experts.

**Example:** A 20-person team saving 7 minutes on 3 searches per day recovers about **140 hours per month**. At a loaded cost of **$30/hour**, that represents up to **$4,200 in monthly productivity value**.

Actual ROI depends on usage, document quality, operating cost, and how saved time is reinvested. A short pilot can validate the value using real search time, answer quality, and adoption data.
